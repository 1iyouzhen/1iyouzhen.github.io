## What is this blog about?

This blog collects learning notes on **LLMs and AI infrastructure**, including paper reading, concepts, code reading, and engineering practice. The articles document a learning process and may be revised as my understanding develops.

## When did you start? How often do you publish?

I started documenting these notes in **2026**. New articles are added as I learn and experiment, without a fixed publishing schedule.

## How do I switch between English and Chinese?

Click the current-language button in the header and select **English** or **简体中文**. When an article has a published translation, the switch opens the corresponding version. Otherwise, it opens the destination language’s home page.

The two versions are maintained separately, so their content and update times may differ.

## How can I find articles or subscribe?

Browse the [archive](/archives/) by date, explore [tags](/tags/) by topic, or use [search](/search/) to find keywords in article titles and content.

Subscribe to the English edition through [RSS](/index.xml). The Chinese edition has a [separate RSS feed](/zh/index.xml).

## How can I report an error or get in touch?

Feedback and corrections are welcome through [Issues in the blog repository](https://github.com/1iyouzhen/1iyouzhen.github.io/issues). Please include the article link, the relevant passage, an explanation, and sources or reproduction steps where possible.

Related code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/1iyouzhen). See the [about page](/about/) for more information.

## How should I cite an article?

Please include the author, article title, and original link. Since articles may be updated, recording the date you accessed a page is helpful. Papers, images, and third-party code retain their original licenses. For full republication or adaptation, please get in touch through the repository’s Issues first.
