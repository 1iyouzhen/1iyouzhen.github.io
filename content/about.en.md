Hello, I’m **Youzhen Li**, [@1iyouzhen](https://github.com/1iyouzhen) on GitHub.

I’m interested in **LLMs and AI infrastructure**. This blog collects my learning notes, paper reading, and engineering practice.

## What I write about

- **Models and algorithms**: starting from a problem, working through the key concepts, assumptions, and derivations.
- **Inference and systems**: the computation, memory, and scheduling behind running models.
- **Engineering and practice**: code reading, reproduction steps, debugging, and conclusions that can be checked.

## About this site

The design follows a simple, reading-focused structure. The articles and other content here are maintained independently. These are learning notes and may contain incomplete explanations. Suggestions and corrections are welcome through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/1iyouzhen/1iyouzhen.github.io/issues).
