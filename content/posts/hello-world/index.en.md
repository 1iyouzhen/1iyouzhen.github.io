This site focuses on LLMs and AI infrastructure. It keeps track of questions worth revisiting, along with the process of reading, deriving, and implementing.

## Why write things down?

Reading a paper or running a piece of code is different from explaining the design clearly. Writing is a way to check understanding: What is the problem? What are the assumptions? What does a conclusion depend on? What remains unclear?

A learning note can start with a small question. A clear explanation, traceable sources, and steps that can be run again are more useful than trying to cover everything.

## How the articles are organized

Future technical notes will aim to answer a few concrete questions:

1. **Problem**: Why do we need this method or system?
2. **Intuition**: How does it work, with as few concepts as possible?
3. **Details**: What are the key derivations, data flows, or code paths?
4. **Verification**: How can we check our understanding and reproduce a result?
5. **Limits**: When does a conclusion stop applying? What questions remain?
